About Aarogya Sahayak

Aarogya Sahayak – Affordable Rural Diagnostic Assistant aims to make healthcare more accessible for rural communities by providing early diagnostics, cost awareness, and essential medical guidance.

Problem We Solve

Many rural areas lack affordable diagnostic tools, proper information, and quick access to medical facilities. Our platform brings basic diagnostics closer to people using simple technology.
